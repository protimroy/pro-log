## ALOPEX Research Thread

*Research series · Updated November 22, 2026*

**Can perturbation-based correlation provide useful neural-network credit assignment without the full machinery of backpropagation, and at what cost?**

This thread separates a focused investigation of activation-space measurements from a broader reconstruction of the ALOPEX optimization program. The articles are distinct pieces of evidence, not independent confirmations of a general training breakthrough.

## Read the articles

1. **[Activation-Space ALOPEX](/pro-log/activation-space-alopex/)** — How hidden-activation interventions, paired consequence measurements, and local evidence were tested. Includes bounded experiments and important comparisons with gradient-based controls.
2. **[ALOPEX: A Partial Negative Result](/pro-log/alopex-partial-negative-result/)** — A wider synthesis of the historical line, experimental branches, scaling questions, and the limits of the measured results.

## Evidence and limitations

The articles distinguish measured observations from hypotheses, incomplete experiments, and historical reconstruction. The results do not establish ALOPEX as a competitive general-purpose substitute for backpropagation.

The source repository is [alopex_2026](https://github.com/protimroy/alopex_2026). Read each article for its specific experimental protocol and evidence boundaries.
